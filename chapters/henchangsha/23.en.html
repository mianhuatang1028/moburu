Lushu

Chapter Twenty-Three: Lushu

Translation first published: 2026-11-10 09:40 (UTC+8) First published here: 2026-11-09 21:27 (UTC+8) © 2026 Moburu · All rights reserved
“Lushu” — In the Classic of Mountains and Seas (“Southern Mountains”), on Mount Niuyang there is a beast shaped like a horse with a white head, a red tail and tiger stripes; its call is like singing, and whoever wears its pelt will be blessed with descendants. As the saying goes: wear it and be blessed with children — see it and start to wonder.

Old Zheng brought Zhang Hailu to Beijing. Early that day the two of them, dressed neatly, walked together to the Sixth Academy’s office building. In front of the entrance a little red car was parked, impossible to miss.

Nothing could have been more familiar to Old Zheng than that car. It was where he and Diao Kexin had made love for the first time. On days beyond counting the two of them had kissed, held each other and poured their hearts out in its back seat. And many, many times he had driven that car with Diao Kexin in the passenger seat beside him — sometimes sleeping sweetly like a child, sometimes holding his hand the whole way and chattering nonstop while he drove. Diao Kexin required that even when Old Zheng was driving he keep one hand holding hers; the moment he let go she’d pout and put on a cross, sulky face.

As he walked into the office building, Old Zheng looked back once more at the car’s driver’s seat. He could see it very clearly: there was no one in it.

That day they had a very in-depth discussion with the client. The materials they’d prepared were without a doubt a great success, and so was the presentation; leaders from several departments gave their discussion direct, positive approval. Because the whole meeting ran long and the client’s questions kept coming, in the window when everyone was deep in discussion they even ate boxed lunches with the client in the meeting room.

By the time they walked out of the building it was already two or three in the afternoon.

But the red Audi was still where it had been.

Old Zheng’s heart began to shudder violently — a strange feeling, excitement and pain mixed together. He said to Zhang Hailu, “Mr. Hailu, you go on ahead. I think I left something behind; I’ll go back and look for it.”

Zhang Hailu left. Old Zheng pretended to walk back into the office building. After a while he came out again and went over to the red Audi. He stood off the car’s left rear side, near the planted strip, lit a cigarette, and kept glancing at the building’s exit.

Half an hour later, sure enough, Diao Kexin came out of that building. It was the first time they’d seen each other in four months, since their last phone call. It was early spring now, April, and Diao Kexin looked very different today from before as she came toward him in a loose, roomy trench coat.

In all this time without seeing her, without a single word from her, she seemed to have filled out all over today — he reckoned she’d put on at least ten kilos — and she wore no makeup at all, completely bare-faced. The Diao Kexin of before wore well-cut trench coats and sharp, slender black or red heels, painted her brows and did her makeup with care whenever she went out, every lipstick brighter than the last. But today she looked like a forlorn housewife, in a loose, shapeless trench coat, as if she’d just walked out of the wet market. Her expression was listless too, her eyelids not even raised. She didn’t seem to see Old Zheng standing behind the car on the left, and kept walking along absorbed in herself; you couldn’t tell from her face whether she was happy or not.

Old Zheng stared hard at Diao Kexin, until she reached her car door. The moment she was about to open it and get in, Old Zheng called out, “Little Ke!”

Diao Kexin turned her head.

Today Old Zheng was wearing a slim black trench coat, a white shirt and tie, sharply pressed suit trousers, black leather shoes shined like new, and he’d changed to a pair of gold-rimmed glasses. He was more than five kilos lighter even than when the two of them had been together, and he looked completely different from before.

The moment Diao Kexin turned and saw Old Zheng, she broke into a wide grin.

She walked a few steps over, stopped a meter from Zheng Zhiyong, and said, “Brother Yong, what are you doing here?”

Zheng Zhiyong: “I’m here for work.”

Diao Kexin: “You’ve changed jobs?”

Zheng Zhiyong: “I’m running my own company now. Xinghuan — they asked me to be general manager and gave me shares. Do you remember it?”

Diao Kexin: “That qualifications company in Xi’an?”

Zheng Zhiyong: “That’s the one. They still have a final payment outstanding with your company, don’t they?”

Diao Kexin: “That company — after we delivered those few orders, I already deregistered it.”

Zheng Zhiyong: “I know.”

Diao Kexin: “So there’s no final payment anymore.”

Zheng Zhiyong nodded and said, “How have you been lately?”

Diao Kexin nodded with a smile and said, “I’m fine.”

Zheng Zhiyong: “Did you get married?”

Diao Kexin: “I did.”

Zheng Zhiyong: “So you got what you wished for?”

Diao Kexin: “I’ve got my reasons, and I’ve got my difficulties too. But sure, you could say I got what I wished for.”

Zheng Zhiyong: “Congratulations.”

Diao Kexin let out a string of soft little laughs through her nose, then said, “Brother Yong, you’ve got to keep it up, okay? You’ve got to build a really, really big company.”

Zheng Zhiyong: “Are you happy?”

Diao Kexin smiled and nodded.

Diao Kexin said, “Brother Yong, are you doing all right?”

Zheng Zhiyong: “I’m doing great. The company’s going smoothly, my shareholders are really backing me — money, resources. We’ve got a team of ten now and we’re working on a ten-million project.”

Diao Kexin’s face lit up with delighted surprise, and she praised him in an exaggerated tone, “That’s amazing, Brother Yong!”

Zheng Zhiyong wanted to step closer to Diao Kexin. He took one step closer, and then a second. Diao Kexin didn’t back away. Now the distance between them had closed to less than thirty centimeters.

Diao Kexin tipped her head back and looked steadily at Zheng Zhiyong with a captivating smile. The way she looked, smiling, was no different at all from those sweet moments when the two of them had been most inseparable.

She stared straight at Zheng Zhiyong and said, “Brother Yong, you look so handsome today. So handsome, so irresistible.”

Diao Kexin had said those words to Old Zheng countless times when they were together, but back then she would always go on to say, “I want to eat you up, like eating a sweet piece of chocolate cake.” And Zheng Zhiyong never had the slightest resistance to Diao Kexin when she said that.

But today, after she said the first part, she didn’t say the second.

Inside, Old Zheng had long since been churning like rivers and seas. He wanted to reach out his right hand and take hold of Diao Kexin, but in an instant the hand he’d put out hesitated, and he pushed it into the pocket of his trench coat.

Then he said, putting on a casual air, “How’s your company doing these days?”

Diao Kexin: “The company isn’t going very smoothly. There are some problems, but they should all be solvable.”

Old Zheng said, “How are the two of you?”

Diao Kexin: “He’s good to me. More considerate than before.”

Old Zheng: “So did you get what you wanted?”

When he asked this, what Old Zheng had in mind was Gao Fei’s billion-yuan fortune, which Diao Kexin had set her heart on through all of the past seven years.

Diao Kexin put away her smile and the look she’d been giving Old Zheng. Her expression turned a little serious, and at the same time she nodded heavily four times.

The rivers and seas inside Old Zheng surged even higher. At that moment his determination to defeat Gao Fei rose up in him. “Fuck it, I’m going to beat Gao Fei. I’m going to beat the business empire that belongs to the two of you.”

Zheng Zhiyong was screaming this hysterically deep inside, but his face didn’t show a thing. He collected himself and said, “Then I wish you well!”

Zheng Zhiyong added, “I’ve written you a lot of emails. If you open your inbox, you’ll see them.”

Diao Kexin gave no answer at all. After a pause she said, “Brother Yong, I have to go. Take care of yourself.”

Diao Kexin turned and in a few steps reached the car door. As she got in she turned her head, looked at Zheng Zhiyong and said again, “You’ve got to keep working hard on your career, okay? You’ve got to make it, got to make it bigger than Yuanwang. Remember what I told you before.”

Old Zheng watched Diao Kexin’s little car drive off without a backward glance, and a riddle slowly rose up in his mind. Looking at her figure today, her makeup, her expression, Old Zheng couldn’t help thinking: “This woman — could she be pregnant? And this child — is there any chance it’s mine, Old Zheng’s?”
